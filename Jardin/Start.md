## 🪴 April — Soil Preparation & Infrastructure

Once the ground thaws and soil is workable (not waterlogged):

### 🌍 Soil Work
- [ ] Do the **squeeze test** first — soil should crumble, not form a sticky ball
- [ ] **Lightly fork** all beds to 20–25 cm — do **not** deep-turn the clay
- [ ] **Deep-fork Root Bed B** to ~30 cm and work in sand/compost (needed for carrots & parsnips)
- [ ] **Topdress all beds** with 3–5 cm of compost/manure
- [ ] **Mulch all paths** with 5–10 cm of hay

### 🏗️ Infrastructure
- [ ] **Build or repair** deer/hare perimeter fencing around the whole garden
- [ ] **Install net support hoops** over Root Bed A, Root Bed B, and Brassica Bed
- [ ] Clear and level the **Trellis Raised Beds**; refresh the growing medium
