# Jardin — Vegetable Garden Plan 2026

## Project Brief

A first-season vegetable garden on a **6 × 10 m** plot in **Zone 4, northern Scandinavia**. The design prioritises hardy, low-maintenance crops suited to a short growing season and heavy clay soil. Beds are sized for clear rotation tracking and manageable upkeep in weekly sessions.

This garden is the opening phase of a broader property vision that may expand into agroforestry and perennial food systems over coming years. Crop choices and bed placement have been made with that long-term direction in mind.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Plan date** | 2026-03-13 |
| **Climate zone** | 4 — northern Scandinavia |
| **Frost-free window** | Approx. late May to late September (confirm locally) |
| **Soil** | Heavy clay, amended with compost and manure |
| **Watering** | Manual hose at regular intervals (first-year baseline) |
| **Growing area** | ~27 m² across five bed zones |
