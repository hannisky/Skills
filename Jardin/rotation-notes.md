# Rotation Notes (2–3 year horizon)

Goal: avoid planting the same crop family in the same bed year after year.
We will plan year-by-year based on results; this is a light framework.

## Crop families
- Potatoes: Solanaceae (but we are only doing potatoes this year).
- Carrots/parsnips: Apiaceae.
- Brassicas: Brassicaceae.
- Alliums: Amaryllidaceae.
- Beets/turnips/rutabaga: Amaranthaceae/Brassicaceae.

## 2026 bed assignments
- Bed A: Potatoes.
- Bed B: Carrot + parsnip.
- RB1: Brassicas.
- RB2: Greens (small).
- RB3: Alliums.
- Right Ground Bed: Mixed trials (root veg + brassica tests).
- Right Raised Bed: Salad/herbs (small).

## Simple rotation idea (2027–2028)
- Move potatoes to a different bed.
- Move carrots/parsnips to a bed that did not have Apiaceae.
- Move brassicas to a bed that did not have Brassicaceae.
- Keep alliums separate and rotate away from last year’s spot.

## Notes
- Because beds are fixed sizes, rotation may be partial or split.
- Focus on avoiding repeating potatoes and brassicas in the same bed.
- Update this file after the 2026 harvest.
