# Tasks (by phase)

## Phase 1: Setup (March–April)
- Confirm bed positions and paths.
- Mark beds with stakes and string.
- Build or repair deer/hare fencing.
- Install net supports over open beds.
- Source manure, compost, hay mulch.

## Phase 2: Soil prep (April–May)
- Work only when soil is not waterlogged.
- Topdress compost/manure (3–5 cm).
- Avoid deep turning in clay.
- Mulch paths and bed edges.

## Phase 3: Planting (May–June)
- Potatoes in Bed A.
- Carrot/parsnip in Bed B.
- Optional: narrow strip of beets/turnips in Bed B if space allows.
- Alliums in west-side allium bed.
- Brassicas in brassica patch.
- Small greens in brassica patch (trial only).
- Salad/microgreens in trellis lidded beds.

## Phase 4: Maintenance (June–Aug)
- Weekly weed control (short sessions).
- Keep nets/fencing in place.
- Hill potatoes twice.
- Water deeply during dry spells.

## Phase 5: Harvest + reset (Aug–Oct)
- Harvest in batches.
- Replant fast crops if space opens early.
- Topdress compost after harvest.
- Winter mulch all beds.

## Phase 6: Review (Oct–Nov)
- Update observations.md.
- Note pests, yields, and soil behavior.
- Adjust next season’s plan.
