# Task Checklist

Organised by phase. Use this as a field reference — check off items as you go.

---

## Phase 1: Setup (March–April)

- [ ] Finalise bed positions and mark with stakes/string.
- [ ] Build or repair deer/hare perimeter fencing.
- [ ] Install net support hoops over Root Bed A, Root Bed B, and Brassica Bed.
- [ ] Source and stockpile compost, well-rotted manure, and hay mulch.
- [ ] Order seeds: carrot, parsnip, cabbage, kale, broccoli, kohlrabi, spinach, lettuce, mâche, rocket, microgreen mix.
- [ ] Order/confirm sets: shallot sets, garlic cloves (already sprouted).
- [ ] Order supplies: insect netting, replacement lids if needed, stakes.

---

## Phase 2: Soil Preparation (April–May)

- [ ] Wait for soil to dry enough to work (squeeze test: should crumble, not form a sticky ball).
- [ ] Lightly fork all beds to 20–25 cm — do not invert or deep-turn clay.
- [ ] Topdress all beds with 3–5 cm compost/manure.
- [ ] Deep-fork Root Bed B to 30 cm and work in sand/compost for carrots.
- [ ] Mulch all paths with 5–10 cm hay.
- [ ] Clear and level Trellis Raised Beds; refresh growing medium.

---

## Phase 3: Planting (May–June)

- [ ] Direct sow carrots and parsnips in Root Bed B (early May, soil >7 °C).
- [ ] Net Root Bed B immediately after sowing (carrot fly).
- [ ] Plant seed potatoes in Root Bed A (late May, after last frost risk).
- [ ] Transplant brassica seedlings into Brassica Bed.
- [ ] Plant shallot sets and garlic sprouts in Allium Bed.
- [ ] Optionally sow greens trial (spinach, lettuce) in Brassica Bed.
- [ ] Sow mâche, rocket, and microgreens in Trellis Raised Beds.
- [ ] Erect insect netting over Root Bed A and Brassica Bed.

---

## Phase 4: Maintenance (June–August)

- [ ] Hill potatoes — first pass (when foliage is ~15 cm).
- [ ] Hill potatoes — second pass (2–3 weeks later).
- [ ] Thin carrots to 3–4 cm spacing; thin parsnips to 10 cm.
- [ ] Weekly weed patrol (15–30 min sessions).
- [ ] Check and repair netting/fencing weekly.
- [ ] Ventilate trellis-bed lids on warm days.
- [ ] Water deeply during dry spells (soak, don't sprinkle).
- [ ] Succession-sow rocket and microgreens in trellis beds.

---

## Phase 5: Harvest & Reset (August–October)

- [ ] Harvest early ("new") potatoes if desired (late July–August).
- [ ] Main potato harvest before heavy frost.
- [ ] Pull carrots and parsnips after first light frost.
- [ ] Cut brassica heads as they mature; leave kale for late harvest.
- [ ] Cure and store shallots and garlic.
- [ ] Re-sow fast crops in any cleared space (turnips, beets, spinach).
- [ ] Topdress harvested beds with compost.
- [ ] Apply 5–10 cm winter mulch (straw or leaves) on all beds.

---

## Phase 6: Season Close & Forward Planning (October–November)

- [ ] **Plant garlic** for 2027 (autumn planting — October).
- [ ] Remove and store netting, stakes, and supports.
- [ ] Record season results in [observations.md](observations.md).
- [ ] Note pest pressure, yields, and soil behaviour.
- [ ] Update [rotation-notes.md](rotation-notes.md) with 2027 bed assignments.
- [ ] Review [materials.md](materials.md) and note anything to restock.
