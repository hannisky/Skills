# Calendar 2026 (Zone 4, first pass)

Start date: 2026-03-13
Goal: go from turned soil to finished garden through autumn.

## Assumptions
- Frost window: mid/late March through end of October.
- Soil workable: late April to early May (depends on thaw).
- Update dates once local frost data is confirmed.

## March 13–31
- Finalize layout and bed edges.
- Order seed, sets, nets, and covers.
- Source manure, compost, and hay for mulch.

## April 1–30
- When soil is workable:
  - Lightly loosen topsoil in all beds.
  - Add 3–5 cm compost/manure on top.
  - Mulch paths heavily with hay.
- Start onions/leeks indoors if using seed.
- Build or repair fencing and net supports.

## May 1–20
- Direct sow carrots and parsnips in Bed B (if soil temp allows).
- Direct sow beets/turnips/rutabaga in Right Ground Bed.
- Install netting immediately after sowing.

## May 21–June 10 (around last frost)
- Plant potatoes in Bed A.
- Transplant brassicas into brassica patch.
- Plant shallots/garlic into allium bed.
- Direct sow small greens in brassica patch (optional and small batch).
- Sow salad/microgreens in trellis lidded beds.

## June 11–July 15
- Hill potatoes twice.
- Thin carrots/parsnips.
- Weed control: weekly; keep mulch thick.
- Monitor for pests and keep nets on.

## July 16–Aug 31
- Harvest early potatoes if desired.
- If a bed clears early:
  - Re-sow fast crops (turnips, beets, spinach) or add compost and mulch.
- Keep watering during dry periods.

## Sept 1–Oct 15
- Main harvest window.
- Pull carrots/parsnips after first light frost for sweetness.
- Clean beds, topdress compost, add winter mulch.

## Oct 16–Nov 15
- Final cleanup.
- Log results in observations.md.
- Update rotation-notes.md for 2027.
