# Growing Calendar 2026

**Zone 4 · Northern Scandinavia**

---

## Key Dates

| Milestone | Approximate timing |
|---|---|
| Soil workable | Late April – early May (depends on thaw) |
| Last spring frost | Late May (confirm locally) |
| First autumn frost | Late September (confirm locally) |
| Frost-free window | ~4 months |

> Update these dates once local frost history is confirmed for the site.

---

## March 13–31 — Planning & Sourcing

- Finalise layout and mark bed positions.
- Order seeds, sets, netting, and covers.
- Source compost, well-rotted manure, and hay for mulch.
- Confirm fencing materials for deer/hare barrier.

---

## April — Soil Preparation

- **When soil is workable** (not waterlogged):
  - Lightly fork topsoil in all beds — do not deep-turn clay.
  - Topdress 3–5 cm compost/manure on all beds.
  - Mulch paths heavily with hay.
- Build or repair deer/hare fencing around the garden perimeter.
- Install net support hoops over open beds.

---

## May 1–20 — Early Direct Sowing

- Direct sow **carrots and parsnips** in Root Bed B once soil temperature reaches ~7 °C.
- Install fine-mesh netting over Root Bed B immediately after sowing.
- Prepare Brassica Bed with extra compost (heavy-feeder bed).

---

## May 21 – June 10 — Main Planting (around last frost)

- Plant **potatoes** in Root Bed A.
- Transplant **brassicas** (cabbage, kale, broccoli, kohlrabi) into Brassica Bed.
- Plant **shallot sets and garlic sprouts** into Allium Bed.
- Direct sow small **greens trial** (spinach, lettuce) in Brassica Bed if space allows.
- Sow **mâche, rocket, and microgreens** in Trellis Raised Beds.
- Erect insect netting over Root Bed A and Brassica Bed.

---

## June 11 – July 15 — Early Maintenance

- **Hill potatoes** twice as foliage grows (Root Bed A).
- Thin carrots and parsnips to proper spacing (Root Bed B).
- Weekly weed control — keep mulch thick, pull by hand.
- Monitor for pests; keep all nets secured.
- Prop open trellis-bed lids on warm days (>18 °C).

---

## July 16 – August 31 — Mid-Season

- Harvest early potatoes if desired ("new potatoes").
- If a bed or section clears early, re-sow fast crops: turnips, beets, or spinach.
- Otherwise, topdress cleared areas with compost and mulch.
- Water deeply during dry periods — soak roots, don't sprinkle.
- Succession-sow rocket and microgreens in trellis beds.

---

## September 1 – October 15 — Main Harvest

- **Harvest window** for most crops.
- Pull carrots and parsnips **after the first light frost** — cold converts starches to sugar.
- Lift remaining potatoes before heavy frost.
- Cut brassicas as heads mature; kale can stay in ground into late autumn.
- Cure shallots and garlic in a dry, ventilated space for storage.
- Clean harvested beds, topdress with compost, apply winter mulch.

---

## October 16 – November 15 — Closing & Forward Planning

- Final bed cleanup; remove spent netting and supports for storage.
- **Plant garlic cloves for 2027** (autumn planting produces larger bulbs).
- Add winter mulch (straw or leaves, 5–10 cm) on all beds.
- Log the season's results in [observations.md](observations.md).
- Update [rotation-notes.md](rotation-notes.md) with 2027 bed assignments.
