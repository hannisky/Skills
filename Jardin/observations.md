## Entry Template

Copy the block below for each new entry.

```
## YYYY-MM-DD

- **Weather:** [conditions, temperature, notable events]
- **Bed(s):** [which beds were checked or worked]
- **Action:** [what was done]
- **Observations:** [growth, colour, health, soil moisture]
- **Pests / Issues:** [any problems spotted]
- **Harvest:** [what was picked, approximate quantity]
- **Notes for next year:** [anything to change or remember]
```

---

## Tips for Useful Entries

- Log **at least once a week** during active season (May–September).
- Note the **first and last frost dates** you actually observe — these improve future calendars.
- Photograph pest damage when you see it; attach or reference the image.
- Record **what you didn’t do** as well (e.g., “skipped weeding this week — noticed X consequence”).
- At season end, review this log and summarise key takeaways in [rotation-notes.md](rotation-notes.md).
