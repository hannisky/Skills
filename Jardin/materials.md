# Materials & Supplies

Essential items needed for the 2026 season. Quantities are estimates based on the bed plan — adjust where local conditions differ.

---

## Soil Amendments

| Item | Approximate quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Well-rotted manure | ~1.5 m³ | 3–5 cm layer across all beds (~27 m²) |
| Compost (finished) | ~1 m³ | Mixed with manure for topdressing |
| Coarse sand | ~0.1 m³ | For Root Bed B top layer (carrot/parsnip tilth) |
| Hay mulch (paths) | ~2 m³ loose | 5–10 cm across all paths; top up mid-season |
| Straw or leaf mulch | ~1 m³ | Winter mulch for all beds (October) |

---

## Fencing & Netting

| Item | Quantity | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Deer/hare fencing (wire or mesh) | ~32 m linear (perimeter) | Full garden boundary |
| Fence posts or stakes | ~16 (every 2 m) | Support for perimeter fencing |
| Fine-mesh insect netting | ~25 m² | Cover Root Beds A, B, and Brassica Bed |
| Net support hoops (wire or PVC) | ~12–16 | Arch over beds to hold netting off crops |
| Replacement lids (trellis beds) | As needed | For RB 1–3 if existing lids are damaged |
