## Crop-by-Crop Detail

### Trellis Raised Beds (continuous harvest)

| Crop | First harvest | Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mâche | ~4 weeks after sowing | June–October | Cut-and-come-again; succession sow every 2–3 weeks |
| Rocket | ~3–4 weeks after sowing | June–October | Bolts in heat — harvest young; re-sow regularly |
| Microgreens | ~10–14 days after sowing | June–October | Fastest turnaround; harvest as sprouts |

### Brassica Bed

| Crop | Expected harvest | Days to maturity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cabbage | August–September | 80–100 from transplant | Harvest when heads are firm and solid |
| Kale | August–first hard frost | 55–75 from transplant | Improves in flavour after light frost; harvest outer leaves |
| Broccoli | August–September | 60–80 from transplant | Cut main head first; side shoots follow for weeks |
| Kohlrabi | August–September | 50–60 from transplant | Harvest when bulb is 7–10 cm diameter (don't let it get woody) |
| Spinach (trial) | July–August | 40–50 from direct sow | Harvest outer leaves; bolts in long days/heat |
| Lettuce (trial) | July–August | 45–60 from direct sow | Cut young for salad; bolts in summer heat |

### Root Beds

| Crop | Expected harvest | Days to maturity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Potatoes — new | Late July–August | 60–80 | Dig a few early for "new potatoes" |
| Potatoes — main | September–October | 90–120 | Harvest before heavy frost; cure in dark for storage |
| Carrots | September–October | 70–80 from direct sow | Sweeten after first light frost; can stay in ground under mulch |
| Parsnips | October (after frost) | 100–120 from direct sow | Need frost to convert starch to sugar; among the last to harvest |
| Beets (optional) | September–October | 55–65 from direct sow | Harvest at golf-ball to tennis-ball size |
| Turnips (optional) | September–October | 40–60 from direct sow | Harvest young for best flavour |

### Allium Bed

| Crop | Expected harvest | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shallots | August–September | Ready when tops yellow and fall over; cure 2–3 weeks in ventilated shade |
| Garlic | August | Harvest when lower leaves brown but 5–6 green leaves remain; cure 3–4 weeks |

## Storage Notes

| Crop | Storage method | Expected shelf life |
|---|---|---|
| Potatoes | Cool, dark, dry; 4–7 °C | 3–6 months |
| Carrots | Sand box or fridge; or leave in ground under mulch | 2–4 months |
| Parsnips | Leave in ground until needed, or store like carrots | 2–4 months |
| Shallots | Cool, dry, ventilated | 6–12 months |
| Garlic | Cool, dry, ventilated | 6–9 months |
| Cabbage | Cool cellar or fridge | 1–3 months |
| Kale | Harvest fresh; does not store long | Use within a week |
