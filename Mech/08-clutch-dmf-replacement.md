# Clutch & Dual Mass Flywheel (DMF) Replacement — Subaru Outback 2009 (EE20 Diesel)

**Service interval:** On-condition (typically 150,000–200,000 km). Replace if pedal is low, vibrating, or "clunking."  
**Difficulty:** ★★★★☆ — Advanced (Requires transmission removal)  
**Time:** 6–8 hours  
**Part:** Full Clutch Kit with DMF (LUK, Sachs, or Exedy)

---

## Why It Matters

The DMF acts as a harmonic dampener for the diesel's torque pulses. In the EE20 engine, a failing DMF sends micro-vibrations directly into the crankshaft. This is a primary contributor to crankshaft snapping. Replacing a tired DMF is the best insurance for engine longevity.

---

## Action Plan for Mechanic

- **Inspection:** Check for "judder" when taking off and rattling noises at idle that disappear when the clutch is pressed.
- **Replacement:** Remove the 5-speed/6-speed manual transmission. Inspect the input shaft seal while inside.
- **Avoid SMF:** Strictly avoid "Single Mass Flywheel" conversions on this specific year (2009 Euro 4). The engine needs the dampening of the DMF to protect the crank.
- **Torque Specs:** Ensure flywheel bolts are replaced (stretch bolts) and torqued to factory specifications in a star pattern.
