# Manual Gearbox Fluid Change — Subaru Outback 2009 BP

**Service interval:** Every 40,000–60,000 km or every 4 years (many owners do this far too rarely)  
**Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆ — Beginner/Intermediate  
**Time:** 30–45 minutes  
**Fluid:** Gear oil 75W (e.g., Amalie 75W or equivalent manual transmission fluid)  
**Capacity:** Approximately 3.0–3.5 litres (verify for your specific gearbox variant)

> **This guide covers the manual transmission only.** The automatic transmission (TZ1/TG5 variants) uses a different fluid (ATF) and a different procedure.

---

## Why It Matters

Manual gearbox oil lubricates gears, bearings, and synchromesh rings. Old, degraded oil:
- Causes notchy or stiff gear changes (especially 1st–2nd)
- Accelerates synchro wear → eventually causes grinding on gear changes
- Loses viscosity and protective properties over time
- In cold Scandinavian winters, thickened old oil makes gear selection very heavy until warm

---

## Fluid Specification

**Amalie 75W** (as listed in parts) is a single-grade manual transmission/gear oil.  
- Single-grade 75W is acceptable for moderate climates; in very cold conditions (below -10°C consistently), a **75W-90 full synthetic** provides better cold-start protection and smoother shifts.
- Cross-reference: Subaru specifies **GL-4 or GL-4/5** rated gear oil for the manual gearbox.  
- **Do NOT use GL-5 only rated oil in the manual gearbox** — the higher EP additive content in GL-5 can attack the yellow-metal synchromesh components. Use GL-4 or GL-4/5.

> Note: The **Hypoid GL-5 80W-90** listed separately in the parts list is for the *differentials*, not the gearbox — see guide 07.

## Location

The manual gearbox sits behind the engine. The **fill plug** and **drain plug** are on the side/bottom of the gearbox casing. The fill plug is typically above the drain plug.

> **Always loosen the fill plug FIRST before draining** — confirms you can refill before committing to an empty gearbox.

---

## Step-by-Step

### 1. Warm the Oil (Recommended)
- Drive for 10–15 minutes to warm the gearbox oil — warm oil flows faster and carries out more contaminants.
- Allow to cool slightly (5 minutes) — warm not hot.

### 2. Lift and Support
- Safely jack the car and place on jack stands. Ensure it's level or close to level for accurate fill volume.

### 3. Locate Plugs
- Identify the **fill plug** (usually on the side of the gearbox, mid-height) and the **drain plug** (lowest point on the gearbox).
- Clean around both plugs with a wire brush or rag to prevent dirt entering.

### 4. Open the Fill Plug First
- Use a 24mm socket to loosen the **fill plug** (counter-clockwise).
- If it won't budge, do not drain yet — get it freed first. A stuck fill plug means you'd drain the box and be unable to refill.
- Once loose, set aside with a new crush washer ready.

### 5. Drain the Old Oil
- Place drain pan under the drain plug.
- Remove drain plug (counter-clockwise).
- Allow to drain fully — 10–15 minutes.
- Inspect the old oil: dark/black is normal. Metal shavings = cause for concern (have gearbox inspected).

### 6. Clean and Reseal the Drain Plug
- Clean the drain plug, thread on a new crush washer.
- Refit and torque to **44 Nm**.

### 7. Refill with New Oil
- Using an oil pump or syringe, pump fresh 75W gear oil through the fill hole.
- Fill until oil just starts to **weep/trickle out of the fill hole** — this is the correct level (fill to the bottom edge of the fill hole).
- Quantity: approximately 3.0–3.5 litres — stop when it runs out, not at a fixed number.

### 8. Refit the Fill Plug
- Thread in new crush washer.
- Torque fill plug to **44 Nm**.

### 9. Lower and Test Drive
- Lower the car.
- Drive with a full range of gear changes. Shifts should feel smooth, especially once oil warms up.
- Check beneath the gearbox for leaks after the test drive.

## Tips & Cross-Reference Notes

- **Forester SH / Legacy BL manual variants**: same gearbox family, same procedure and fill volume.
- **Impreza GR/GH manual**: same 6-speed gearbox used in STi/WRX shares lineage — same process.
- If you have **cold-weather gear change issues**, switch from 75W to **75W-90 full synthetic GL-4** — the difference in winter is dramatic.
- The Subaru 6-speed manual is known for notchy 1st–2nd shifts when cold; fresh GL-4 75W-90 largely eliminates this.
- Do not overfill — excess oil can pressurize the box and push past seals.

---

## Disposal

Used gear oil is hazardous waste. Store in a sealed container and take to a recycling centre, oil bank, or auto shop for proper disposal.
