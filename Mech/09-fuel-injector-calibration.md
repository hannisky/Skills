# Fuel Injector Calibration & Cleaning — Subaru Outback 2009 (EE20 Diesel)

**Service interval:** Every 50,000 km or if "diesel knock" is heard.  
**Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆ — Intermediate (Requires specialized Software)  
**Time:** 30–45 minutes  
**Part:** High-quality Diesel Injector Cleaner (e.g., Liqui Moly Diesel Spülung)

---

## Why It Matters

Uneven fuel delivery causes "imbalanced combustion," placing uneven stress on the connecting rods and crankshaft. Keeping injectors calibrated ensures the engine runs smoothly and minimises the "shaking" that damages the block.

---

## Parts & Tools

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Software | Subaru SSM3 (Select Monitor) or FreeSSM / RomRaider with Tactrix cable |
| Additive | Specialised injector cleaner (can be run via fuel tank or direct loop) |

---

## Step-by-Step (Diagnostic)

- **Injector Learning:** Have the mechanic perform a "Small Quantity Learning" or "Injector Calibration" via the OBD2 port. This recalibrates the ECU to the wear levels of the injectors.
- **Correction Values:** Check the "Idle Speed Injection Volume" for each cylinder. If one cylinder is significantly higher/lower, that injector may need professional refurbishing.
- **Fuel Filter:** Replace the fuel filter (located in the engine bay) every 40,000 km to prevent debris from reaching the injectors.
