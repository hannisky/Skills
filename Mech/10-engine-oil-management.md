# Engine Oil & Lubrication Management — Subaru Outback 2009 (EE20 Diesel)

**Service interval:** Strictly every 10,000 km (Ignore the 15,000 km factory "long-life" interval).  
**Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆ — Beginner/Intermediate  
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Part:** ACEA C3 Spec 5W-30 Synthetic Oil

---

## Why It Matters

The EE20 engine is prone to Oil Dilution. During DPF regeneration, extra diesel is injected. If the cycle is interrupted, this diesel ends up in the oil pan, thinning the oil and wearing out the crankshaft main bearings.

## Critical Practice

- **Oil Level Monitoring:** Check your dipstick weekly. If the oil level is rising above the "Full" mark, it means diesel is diluting the oil. Change the oil immediately if this happens.
- **Short Trips:** Avoid frequent short trips where the engine never reaches full operating temperature.
