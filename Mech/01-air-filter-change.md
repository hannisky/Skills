# Air Filter Replacement — Subaru Outback 2009 BP (EE20 Diesel / EJ Petrol)

**Service interval:** Every 40,000 km or annually (more often in dusty conditions)  
**Difficulty:** ★☆☆☆☆ — Beginner  
**Time:** 10–15 minutes  
**Part:** Element – Air Cleaner (also fits similar-generation Forester, Legacy, Impreza with same engine family)

---

## Why It Matters

The air filter prevents abrasive dust and debris from entering the engine. A clogged filter restricts airflow, reduces combustion efficiency, and on a diesel can cause excessive smoke, poor fuel economy, and accelerated turbo wear.

---

## Parts & Tools

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Air filter element | OEM Subaru: 16546AA090 (EJ engines) / 16546AA100 (EE20 diesel — verify with VIN) |
| Alternative fit | Subaru Forester SH (2008–2012), Legacy BL/BP same generation |
| Tools | Flat-head screwdriver or 8mm socket (for airbox clips/screws) |

> **Note:** The `{4}` notation in the parts list likely denotes the 4-cylinder engine variant — both EJ25 petrol and EE20 diesel are inline-4s, confirming this filter fits both.

---

## Location

The air filter box (airbox) sits in the **engine bay on the passenger side** (right side when facing the engine). It's a black plastic box connected to the intake ducting and throttle body.

---

## Step-by-Step

### 1. Prepare
- Turn the engine off and let it cool.
- Lift the bonnet/hood and prop it open.

### 2. Open the Airbox
- Locate the rectangular or oval plastic airbox on the passenger side of the engine bay.
- Release the **spring clips or loosen the screws** securing the top lid of the airbox (typically 4 clips or 2–4 screws depending on variant).
- On the EE20 diesel, you may also need to disconnect the **MAF (Mass Air Flow) sensor plug** from the intake duct before lifting the lid — gently squeeze the tab and pull.

### 3. Remove the Old Filter
- Lift the lid and remove the old filter element.
- Look inside the airbox for any debris, leaves, or insects — wipe clean with a dry cloth if necessary.
- **Do not use compressed air to blast debris towards the intake side.**

### 4. Install the New Filter
- Place the new element in the airbox with the **rubber seal facing upward** (sealing against the lid).
- Ensure it sits flat and the seal contacts all the way around.

### 5. Close the Airbox
- Reattach the lid, ensure all clips snap firmly or screws are tightened.
- Reconnect the MAF sensor connector if disconnected.
- Give the intake duct a gentle tug to confirm it's seated.

### 6. Verify
- Start the engine and confirm it idles normally. No unusual noises from the intake area.

---

## Tips & Cross-Reference Notes

- **Forester SH / Legacy BL** (2003–2009 EJ engines): same procedure, same orientation.
- **Impreza GH/GR (2007–2011)**: smaller engine bay but identical clip-style airbox.
- On the **EE20 diesel**, ensure the intake pre-filter (if fitted) is also inspected.
- If the engine is heavily turbocharged or modified, consider a performance drop-in filter (e.g., K&N 33-series equivalent).

---

## Disposal
Old air filters are general waste — not hazardous. Most auto parts stores accept them.
