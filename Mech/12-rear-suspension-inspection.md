# Rear Suspension & Chassis Inspection — Subaru Outback 2009 (BP)

**Service interval:** Annually (especially before winter).  
**Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆ — Intermediate  
**Time:** 1 hour inspection

---

## Why It Matters

The Outback (BP) has a multi-link rear suspension. Rust in the rear subframe or upper control arms can lead to sudden handling failures.

---

## Checkpoints

- **Rear Strut Towers:** Inspect inside the trunk area (pull back the carpet) for any signs of "mushrooming" or rust.
- **Self-Leveling Shocks:** Most Outbacks of this era had self-leveling rear shocks. If the rear looks "saggy" even when empty, the shocks are blown.
  - **Tip:** Replace with heavy-duty standard shocks and stiffer springs (e.g., KYB or Ironman 4x4) for an "explorer" build.
