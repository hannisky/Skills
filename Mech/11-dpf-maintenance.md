# DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) Maintenance — Subaru Outback 2009 (EE20 Diesel)

**Service interval:** Continuous monitoring / Forced regen as needed.  
**Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆ — Intermediate (Software based)  
**Time:** 30–60 minutes (Driving or stationary)

---

## Why It Matters

A clogged DPF creates high backpressure on the turbocharger and increases combustion temperatures, which stresses the engine head and bottom end. At 250,000 km, the DPF is likely reaching its "ash limit."

---

## Action Plan

- **The "Italian Tune-up":** Once a week, drive the car on a highway for 20 minutes at sustained RPMs (above 2,000 RPM) to allow a passive regeneration.
- **Forced Regen:** If the DPF light flashes, have a mechanic perform a "Forced Regeneration" via software.
- **Chemical Cleaning:** Since your mileage is high, consider a professional DPF "off-car" cleaning service to remove accumulated ash (which cannot be burned off by the car).
