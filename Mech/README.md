## Important Notes

### Diesel vs Petrol
The **EE20 diesel** variant has a serviceable external cartridge fuel filter. The EJ petrol variants do not — their fuel filter is integrated in the fuel pump inside the tank and is not a routine service item.

### GL-4 vs GL-5 — Critical
- **Manual gearbox**: GL-4 or GL-4/5 only (75W or 75W-90)
- **Rear differential**: GL-5 80W-90 (hypoid gear oil)
- Using GL-5 in the gearbox damages synchromesh rings over time

### Crush Washers
The 26.3×32.3×1mm gasket is a universal crush washer used across the oil, gearbox, and differential drain plugs. Buy in bulk — always replace, never reuse.

### Brake Fluid
DOT4 is hygroscopic — it must be replaced every 2 years regardless of mileage. This is especially important in humid northern European climates.

## Tools Required (Across All Jobs)

- Standard socket set (8mm, 12mm, 14mm, 24mm)
- Torque wrench (range 8–120 Nm)
- Floor jack + jack stands
- Drain pan (5L minimum)
- Oil pump/filler syringe (for gear oil)
- Vacuum or pressure bleeder (brakes)
- 8mm spanner (brake bleed nipples)
- Filter housing wrench — 65mm (diesel fuel filter)
- Rags, gloves, eye protection

---

## Safety Reminders

1. Always use jack stands — never work under a car supported only by a floor jack
2. Brake fluid strips paint — protect bodywork and wash hands immediately
3. Work with adequate ventilation when handling fuels and flammants
4. Dispose of all fluids and filters responsibly — they are classified hazardous waste
